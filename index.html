The boat hull uses kinematic multi-sensor averaging to filter out high-frequency mechanical vibration and lever-arm deflection. 2. **Automated Stroke-Recovery Zeroing (Zero Tare Buttons)**: * During the boat's recovery phase (when surge acceleration is low and force variance is settled), the background baseline estimator continuously computes the 40th-percentile resting median load for each plate independently. * Deflections are clamped cleanly above baseline. Thermal drift, flipped mounting, and unequal sensor biases are compensated automatically with no manual intervention. 3. **Coherent Multi-Sensor SPM Detection & Audio Synthesizer**: * Analyzes synchronized bow surge spikes, GPS speed velocity movement ($>1.4\text{ km/h}$), and mechanical load rise with a 1.35-second refractory lockout window. * Rejects water slaps, chop, or dock tapping to prevent overestimation of stroke rate. * Synthesizes an 880 Hz crystal sine pulse with exponential decay through the Web Audio API on every genuine catch. 4. **Layered Mobile-First Layout**: * **Layer 1**: 3D dual-IMU kinematics model + ArcGIS live satellite GPS map. * **Layer 2**: Session controls (`START`, `PAUSE`, `END`, `RECALL`, `CALIBRATE`) + Live & Average workout metric cards. * **Layer 3**: Live streaming flow chart showing Plate 1 Force, Plate 2 Force, and clean bow surge. * **Layer 4**: Dedicated individual stroke profile overlays for Plate 1 and Plate 2 featuring ghosted previous strokes, bright active strokes, and running averages (Gold for P1, Cyan for P2). 5. **Strava-Style Basic Recall Modal & Segment Cropper**: * Displays hero summary metrics (Total Time, Distance, Split, SPM, Strokes, and Plate Symmetry Balance). * Includes an interactive Leaflet track map, session overview charts, dual-handle range sliders for cropping, and export options (`EXPORT CROPPED (CSV)`, `EXPORT FULL (CSV)`). * Includes a `PULL FROM SD CARD (BLE)` command trigger to stream logs directly from the ESP32. 6. **Preserved 4-Chart Advanced Analysis Suite**: * Word-for-word preservation of the original 2.5D isometric attitude plate, interactive canvas with mouse wheel/drag zoom and pan, scrolling replay scrubber, flatline spike editor, timed playback engine ($0.25\times$ to $5.0\times$), and the modal with Catch-Aligned, Finish-Aligned, Surge Stretch, and Split-Aligned analysis charts.
